# Friend-test interview guide

Two short interviews per tester: after week 1 (15 minutes) and after week 4 (25 minutes). The app never sends data, so these conversations and the voluntary export are how you learn.

## Before you start

- Remind them: this tests the app, not their parenting. There are no wrong answers.
- Ask if they're happy to share their screen or their "My usage summary". It's fine if not.
- Don't ask for details about their child beyond what they choose to say.

## Week 1 (about 15 minutes)

1. Walk me through the first time you opened it. What did you expect it to be?
2. How did the welcome make you feel? Was anything off-putting?
3. Did the plan make sense for your family? Was any module surprising?
4. Did you set a practice time, a partner, reminders? Why or why not?
5. How did the practice go this week? What got in the way?
6. Did anything nearly make you stop using it?
7. How likely are you to open it next week (0–10)? What would make that higher?

## Week 4 (about 25 minutes)

1. Show me (or tell me) how you used it across the four weeks. Where did it drop off, if it did?
2. Which module helped most? Which least? Why?
3. Practice: what made it easier or harder to do at home? Did the "when" plan help?
4. Practice partner: did you share? What happened? Would you want more, or less, human contact?
5. Reminders: helpful, annoying, ignored?
6. Learning style and short versions: did you use them? Did you switch?
7. "I already do this", "Welcome back", repeats: did you see them? How did they feel?
8. Did anything make you feel judged, guilty or out of your depth?
9. Did anything change at home? (Only what they want to share.)
10. Would you recommend it to another parent? Who, and what would you say?
